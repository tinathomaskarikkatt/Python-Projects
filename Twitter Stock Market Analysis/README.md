# 📈 Twitter Stock Market Analysis using Python

A Python-based exploratory data analysis (EDA) project that examines Twitter's historical stock market performance. The project analyzes stock price trends, volatility, trading volume, and investor behavior using statistical techniques and data visualizations to uncover meaningful financial insights.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project explores Twitter's historical stock market data to understand how its stock price evolved over time. The analysis focuses on identifying price fluctuations, trading volume patterns, market volatility, and investor sentiment through statistical analysis and visualization.

The project demonstrates the complete data analysis workflow—from data preprocessing and exploratory analysis to statistical testing and visual storytelling.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze historical Twitter stock price trends.
- Study stock price volatility over time.
- Examine the relationship between stock prices and trading volume.
- Apply statistical techniques to validate observations.
- Create meaningful visualizations for financial analysis.
- Derive business insights from historical stock data.

# 💡 Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important findings:

- Twitter's stock experienced significant volatility, with frequent fluctuations throughout the observed period.
- Price movements were often accompanied by spikes in trading volume, indicating increased investor activity.
- While the stock showed short-term growth phases, it lacked consistent long-term upward momentum.
- The difference between daily high and low prices widened during volatile periods, reflecting increased market uncertainty.
- Recent years showed greater price instability than earlier periods.
- The analysis suggests that external events and changing investor sentiment had a strong influence on Twitter's stock performance, ultimately contributing to its market instability and eventual delisting.
